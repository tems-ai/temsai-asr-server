How to use a fire extinguisher. If a small fire starts in the workshop, first make sure the alarm has been raised and that everyone else is leaving the area. Only fight the fire if it is small, if you have a clear exit behind you, and if you have the right type of extinguisher. Remember the word PASS. Pull, aim, squeeze and sweep. First, pull the pin at the top of the extinguisher. This breaks the seal so the handle can move. Second, aim the nozzle low, at the base of the fire, not at the flames. The fuel is at the base, so that is where the extinguishing agent has to go. Third, squeeze the handle slowly and evenly to release the agent. Fourth, sweep the nozzle from side to side across the base of the fire until it appears to be out. Keep a safe distance of about two to three metres and move closer only as the fire gets smaller. Watch the area afterwards, because a fire can start again. If the extinguisher is empty and the fire is still burning, leave immediately, close the door behind you and wait for the fire brigade outside. After use, report it to your supervisor so the extinguisher can be refilled or replaced.
